# The free hymn library

`hymns.json` is built by `scripts/build-hymnal.mjs` from:

- **WorshipCommons** (https://worshipcommons.org, https://github.com/ChurchApps/WorshipCommonsContent):
  English songs that are in the public domain (in the United States, as
  WorshipCommons judges it, best-effort) or under a Creative Commons
  Attribution licence. Each song's `credit` names its writer and licence; the
  app shows it with the song. Songs under the WorshipCommons License or a
  writer's own permission are not included: those grants cover a church's
  worship, not handing the songs out in an app. WorshipCommons' catalog and
  tools are MIT-licensed.
- **The Open Hymnal Project** (http://openhymnal.org, via
  https://github.com/mzealey/openhymnal): public-domain hymn scores, for
  hymns WorshipCommons does not have, and the scores (also used by
  WorshipCommons) that every verse is read from.

No warranty: copyright decisions were made in good faith by those projects and
may be wrong for some songs or countries.
